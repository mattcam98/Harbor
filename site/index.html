Harbor

Install and run your own apps on iPhone with a free Apple ID. Harbor signs and renews them for you.

Free · iOS 17 or later

Harbor is installed from a Mac, once. Open this page on your Mac:

Install

  1. Install from your Mac

    You'll need a Mac with Apple silicon, a cable and your Apple ID. On the iPhone, turn on Developer Mode in Settings › Privacy & Security.

    Connect and unlock the iPhone, then paste this into Terminal on the Mac:

    /bin/bash -c "$(curl -fsSL install.sh)"

    Harbor Setup checks its download, signs Harbor with your Apple ID and installs it. Your password goes only to Apple and isn't stored.

  2. Trust it on the iPhone

    In Settings › General › VPN & Device Management, tap your Apple ID, then Trust. Install LocalDevVPN from the App Store and turn it on.

  3. Let it renew itself

    A free Apple ID signs apps for 7 days. Harbor renews itself on the iPhone: tap Renew, or set up a daily Shortcuts automation in Settings › Automatic Renewal.

    On iOS 27 and later, Harbor can also pair with the iPhone by itself, so you won't need the Mac again.
