Harbor

Install, sign, refresh and run sideloaded apps with your own Apple ID, from the iPhone.

Free · iOS 17 or later

Harbor is installed from a Mac, once. Open this page on your Mac:

Install

  1. Install from a Mac, once

    You need a Mac with Apple silicon, the iPhone's cable and your Apple ID. On the iPhone, turn on Developer Mode (Settings › Privacy & Security).

    Connect the iPhone to the Mac and unlock it. Open Terminal on the Mac and paste:

    /bin/bash -c "$(curl -fsSL install.sh)"

    Harbor Setup downloads, checks itself against the digest below, asks for your Apple ID, signs Harbor with it and installs it on the iPhone. Your password goes only to Apple and is not stored.

  2. Trust it on the iPhone

    Settings › General › VPN & Device Management: tap your Apple ID and Trust. Install LocalDevVPN from the App Store and connect it.

  3. After that, no computer

    Harbor renews itself on the iPhone before the seven days a free Apple ID allows run out: tap Renew Harbor, or let a daily Shortcuts automation do it.

    Harbor Setup also gives Harbor this iPhone's pairing record over the cable, which is what lets it install and renew.

    iOS 27 and later In testing

    If the pairing record is ever lost, Harbor can make a new one on the iPhone, without the Mac: This device › Pair this iPhone, then Pair with Harbor in Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode.
